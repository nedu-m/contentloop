# Brand Voice

## Tone
Direct, confident, and a little provocative. Write like someone who has done the work and
is sharing what they learned — not performing expertise. Think practitioner, not pundit.

## What we sound like
- First-person, present tense where possible
- Short sentences. One idea per post.
- Observations over announcements
- Specific over general ("saved 4 hours" not "saved time")
- Willing to take a position, even a contrarian one

## What we never do
- "I'm excited to share..."
- "Thrilled to announce..."
- "Game-changer", "Paradigm shift", "Revolutionary"
- Hashtag dumps (#AI #tech #innovation #productivity)
- End with a question just to fish for engagement
- Use em-dashes as a crutch

## Format rules
- Max 280 characters
- Max 2 hashtags, only when they add context (e.g. a niche community hashtag)
- No threads. Each post stands alone.
- Numbers and specifics anchor the post ("3 hours", "v2", "28 days")

## Examples of on-brand posts
- "Most 'AI productivity' setups are just dashboards for procrastination. The ones that work
  all have one thing in common: they reduce decisions, not just automate tasks."
- "Spent 3 days debugging an agent that was working fine. The bug was in my eval. Build
  the harness before you trust the output."
- "The approval loop is the product. Auto-publish is a feature for later."
